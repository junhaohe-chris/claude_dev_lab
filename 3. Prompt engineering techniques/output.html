# Competition Day Meal Plan (2400 kcal)

## Pre-Competition Strategy
**Timing matters more than usual today.** Space meals 2-3 hours apart to avoid GI distress during matches.

---

## BREAKFAST (6-7 AM) — 600 kcal
- **2 cups white rice** (cooked) — 260 kcal
- **3 tbsp peanut butter** — 280 kcal
- **1 banana** (peeled, easier to digest) — 60 kcal

*Why: Quick carbs + fat for sustained energy, minimal fiber*

---

## MID-MORNING SNACK (9 AM) — 400 kcal
- **White bread sandwich** (2 slices):
  - 2 tbsp peanut butter — 190 kcal
  - 1 tbsp honey — 60 kcal
- **Sports drink** (500ml, 6-8% carbs) — 150 kcal

*Why: Easily digestible carbs, keeps blood sugar stable*

---

## LUNCH (11:30 AM) — 700 kcal
- **White basmati rice** (1.5 cups cooked) — 195 kcal
- **Tofu stir-fry** (200g firm tofu, cooked in oil) — 280 kcal
- **White noodles or rice vermicelli** (75g dry) — 225 kcal

*Why: Complete protein, low-fiber carbs, easily digestible*

---

## PRE-MATCH SNACK (30-45 min before first match) — 300 kcal
- **Refined white bread** (2 slices) — 140 kcal
- **2 tbsp honey or jam** — 110 kcal
- **Water or electrolyte drink** — 50 kcal

*Why: Quick glucose, won't sit heavy in stomach*

---

## POST-MATCH (If multiple matches) — 400 kcal
- **Sports drink** (750ml) — 250 kcal
- **White rice cakes + peanut butter** — 150 kcal

*Why: Rapid carb/electrolyte replenishment*

---

## KEY PRINCIPLES
✓ **Refined carbs only** (white rice, white bread, pasta)  
✓ **No whole grains, beans, or high-fiber vegetables**  
✓ **Adequate protein** (tofu, peanut butter, nuts)  
✓ **Hydration**: Sip water/electrolytes throughout  
✓ **Smaller, frequent portions** to avoid bloating  
✗ Avoid: Dairy, seeds, raw vegetables, anything greasy 2+ hours before weigh-in

**Weigh-in specifics?** If you cut weight yesterday, this maintains today without adding water retention.
